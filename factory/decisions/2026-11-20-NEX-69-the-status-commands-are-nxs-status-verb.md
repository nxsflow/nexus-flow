# 2026-11-20 · NEX-69 · The status commands are nxs status draft, write, list, show and waiting

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: one noun and a verb, after the module's name, as `nxs ticket` and `nxs decision` are (RFC 0007, "Public surfaces this RFC brings"). Answer (a) in NEX-69.
- Instead of: `nxs note <verb>`, which a ticket's notes make ambiguous; or a bare `nxs status` that also prints the newest note.
- Topics: cli, status
