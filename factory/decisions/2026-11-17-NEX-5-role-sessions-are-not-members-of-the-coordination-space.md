# 2026-11-17 · NEX-5 · Role sessions are not members of the coordination space

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: pm ranks the work against the goals and turns requests into tickets, so a coder sees a goal only as the reference its ticket holds, and no second membership is added and removed per session (RFC 0001, "Roles in a space"). A later version may give a coder the coordination-space access pm names (NEX-40), which is easier now that a coder is pm's sub-agent. Answer 5a in NEX-38.
- Instead of: every role session as a reader of the coordination space.
- Topics: members, coordination, nocentraldb
