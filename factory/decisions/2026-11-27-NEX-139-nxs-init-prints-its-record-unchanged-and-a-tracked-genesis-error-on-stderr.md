# 2026-11-27 · NEX-139 · nxs init prints its record unchanged, and a tracked GENESIS's error on stderr

- Decided by: coder on NEX-139; provisional until the owner answers NEX-243, what `nxs init` prints of the two installs
- Why: neither RFC 0001, RFC 0004 nor RFC 0006 says what `nxs init` prints about the factory and GENESIS it installs, and its output is a surface the owner decides. So the safe subset: `nxs init`'s JSON and Markdown stay as NEX-97 and NEX-229 made them, and the reports are not printed; the question is NEX-243, for the owner. The one change follows from RFC 0004, where `nxs genesis create` exits non-zero on a `tracked` GENESIS: `nxs init` then prints its record on stdout as always, writes the same `error: …` that `nxs genesis create` writes to stderr, in JSON and in Markdown alike, and exits 1, as NEX-133 decided for `nxs genesis create`. The space and the factory are already in place then, and a rerun after restoring GENESIS from git finishes the run.
- Instead of: adding `factory` and `genesis` keys to the record, a new output surface decided in a coding PR; or v1's error object on stdout, which would drop the record of a space that was created.
