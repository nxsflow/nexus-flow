# 2026-11-20 · NEX-6 · A closed ticket's resolution is done, canceled or replaced

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: `replaced` covers a duplicate and a ticket folded into another, since both mean the work continues in the ticket it names; the closing comment says which (RFC 0002, "Statuses, closing and reopening"). Answer to NEX-56.
- Instead of: `done`, `canceled`, `duplicate` and `merged_into`, as the NoCentralDB factory used them in Linear.
- Topics: tickets, states
