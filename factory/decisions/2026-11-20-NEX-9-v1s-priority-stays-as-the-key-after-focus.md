# 2026-11-20 · NEX-9 · v1's priority stays, as the key after the goal in focus

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: goals rank by what the group pursues, priority by how urgent one ticket is; a broken build is urgent whatever the goals are, and v1's data keeps its meaning. `next` orders by status, the position of the ticket's goal in focus, then priority (RFC 0005, "What v1's priority becomes"). Answer to NEX-60.
- Instead of: deprecating priority, so that goals alone rank and type, due date and key order the rest.
