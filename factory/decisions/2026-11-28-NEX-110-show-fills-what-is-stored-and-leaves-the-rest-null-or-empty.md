# 2026-11-28 · NEX-110 · nxs ticket show fills what the ticket stores and leaves derived state, reverse edges, notes, work and references null or empty

- Decided by: coder on NEX-110; the shape of the key lists and the Markdown layout are provisional until the owner answers NEX-251
- Why: the ticket asks for `show` with the basic record: every key present, `[]` and `null` for empty values. `show` fills every stored field, `parent` as its epic's key, `deps` from `dependsOn` (each `{key, status, done, on_main}`, with `done` and `on_main` `null` until NEX-118), and `children` from `byParent`, since `create` writes both edges. The derived `ready`, `blocked`, `deferred`, `done`, `on_main`, `focus` and `focus_goal` are `null` and `flags` empty until NEX-118, NEX-119 and RFC 0005's focus land; `dependents`, `mentioned_by` and `replaces`, walks over the graph, are empty until NEX-119 and NEX-112; `contributes_to` and `mentions`, which no command writes yet, are empty until NEX-111 and NEX-112; `notes`, `work` and `references` are empty until NEX-115, NEX-114 and NEX-116; `request` is `null` until RFC 0009's field exists. `children`, `contributes_to`, `mentions`, `mentioned_by` and `replaces` are lists of keys as `nxs` shows them, in key order (tag, then number), like `parent`; RFC 0002 gives no shape for them, nor a layout for `show --md`, which is a heading, the fields that are set, then the description, design and completion criterion; both wait for the owner in NEX-251.
- Instead of: leaving `deps` and `children` empty too, which would show a ticket's own edges as missing right after `create` wrote them; or objects like `deps` for every list, a shape the RFC names only for `deps` and `dependents`.
- Topics: tickets, derived-state, nxs, v2
