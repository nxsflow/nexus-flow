# 2026-11-20 · NEX-50 · The request and coordination commands keep their proposed names

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: `nxs request <verb>` for requests, and `nxs space create-coordination` and `leave-coordination` beside RFC 0001's `join-coordination`, keep the space commands together under `nxs space`; `nxs repository list` lists the group. Written into RFC 0009, "Public surfaces this RFC brings".
- Instead of: nouns of their own, such as `nxs coordination create`, `join` and `leave`.
- Topics: cross-repo, coordination, cli
