# 2026-11-17 · NEX-5 · A session's invitation reaches its commissioner as a session message, with a ticket comment as the fallback

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: the invitation holds no secret, and a cloud session cannot reliably message back today, so the fallback is needed (RFC 0001, "Starting a session's member"). Answer 4a in NEX-38.
- Instead of: always a ticket comment.
- Topics: members, sessions, nocentraldb
