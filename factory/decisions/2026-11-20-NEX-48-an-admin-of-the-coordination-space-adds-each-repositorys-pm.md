# 2026-11-20 · NEX-48 · An admin of the coordination space adds each repository's pm

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: RFC 0001's roles stay as they are (other developers' devices are writers there), so admins stay few; in a group of the owner's repositories the admin is always the owner. The owner asked for pm replacement to be made easier in a later version. Written into RFC 0009, "Members and roles" and "What it does not do".
- Instead of: making the developer who commissions each repository's pm an admin of the coordination space, who could then also remove the other repositories' members.
- Topics: cross-repo, coordination, members
