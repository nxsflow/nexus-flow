# 2026-11-20 · NEX-65 · A group of repositories gets no status note of its own: each repository writes its note into its own space

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: a note in the repository space links to its tickets and decisions, and the owner reads a group's notes of a day together through an index across the spaces they belong to (RFC 0007, "Where a note lives"). Answer (a) in NEX-65.
- Instead of: a summary note per group in the coordination space beside the repositories' notes, or a note in the coordination space only.
- Topics: status, nocentraldb, spaces
