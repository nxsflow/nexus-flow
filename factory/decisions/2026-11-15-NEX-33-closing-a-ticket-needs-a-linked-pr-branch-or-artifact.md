# 2026-11-15 · NEX-33 · Closing a ticket needs a linked PR or branch, or, for a role that works outside the repository, a linked artifact

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: roles such as legal or marketing hold lanes like a coder but produce no PR; a linked artifact is their bridge to "done". Answer to the review in NEX-30.
- Instead of: requiring a linked PR or branch for every ticket, which a role without code could never meet.
