# 2026-11-15 · NEX-33 · goals and tickets are full modules of nexus-flow, not adapters to another tracker

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: nexus-flow keeps tickets and goals itself, in NoCentralDB, so agents read them locally and the factory does not depend on Linear or GitHub. Answer to the review in NEX-30.
- Instead of: adapters to Linear, GitHub Issues or beads for tickets, goals and status, as the NEX-30 review proposed.
- Topics: goals, tickets, modules
