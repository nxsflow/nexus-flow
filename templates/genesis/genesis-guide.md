# How to fill in GENESIS.md

GENESIS.md says what this product is, what we build, for whom, its principles, its no-gos and its
trade-offs. The owner writes it; agents read it before any work.

The list under the mark `<!-- nexus-flow: owner-rules -->` holds the rules on what comes back to
the owner. Keep the mark, and write one rule per item.
